Gift Card FAQ

A few common questions

How does the recipient redeem it?

They book any Forest Park Picnics experience at forestparkpicnics.com and apply the gift card code at checkout. Easy as that.

Does the gift card expire?

Never. Use it this season, next season, or save it for a special occasion years from now.

Can the value be split across multiple bookings?

Yes — any unused balance stays on the card. Lunch on Art Hill in May, dinner at Pagoda Lake in July, dessert wherever the rest takes you.

When is the gift card delivered?

Choose any send date during checkout. The recipient gets a beautifully designed email with the code and a link to book.

Is it just for picnics?

Yes — gift cards are redeemable on any Forest Park Picnics experience: Art Hill lunches (April–October) or Pagoda Lake dinners (May–September).
